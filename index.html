Silence Can Speak.

After winning a short film in an intercollegiate, the human vice called “ambition” took over me. And I soon convinced the team to make another one, because it was aching not to. And i learned, passion is contagious. Or to be precise, my passion.

We shot it in a day inside the campus, skipping our classes and i edited and surprised the team with the final product the next day. We shared it amongst friends, who we thought wouldn’t dismiss the idea of silent black and white, experimental film at the first glance.

One of them, stating ”What in the slavic art film is this. Brilliant. It’s about expression. An aura of expression emanates from the character but is invisible to others. The world around him brings it out and because of that in a way sees the inanimate world does see it. Things complement it but nothing reacts to it. Mimesis of colorfulness in black and white.”

At the end, We were happy. With the fact that something can be done. At least, something.
